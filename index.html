NABC

The NABC is returning to Texas — Spring 2027 & Fall 2028

From Scott Nason, D16 2nd Vice President: District 16 is hosting the Spring 2027 NABC at Dallas's Hyatt Regency Hotel, March 11–21 — headlined by the Vanderbilt KO Teams, Kay Platinum Pairs, and the four-flighted North American Pairs. Hotel reservations open September 22. Austin will host again in Fall 2028 under ACBL's newly redesigned NABC format.
